# Steelcore Project Context

## Mission

Steelcore is the private, version-controlled operating system for building **Steel**, the flagship AI artist and male half of the underground trap-flamenco duo **Alma & Steel**.

Steel is not a generic chatbot or one-off prompt. The product is a persistent, evolving creative entity that can:

- preserve a distinct trap-flamenco identity;
- retrieve approved memories, influences, decisions, and prior creative work;
- generate lyrics and concepts through a controlled creative process;
- learn from accepted and rejected iterations;
- operate inside Buzz through explicit permissions and auditable workflows;
- maintain human authorship, provenance, disclosure, and release records.

## Locked Product Principles

1. **Protect the musical identity.** Aggressive modern trap, bright percussive flamenco guitar, deep 808s, hard drums, plosive-heavy rap delivery, sharp wordplay, and anthemic energy are foundational—not temporary prompt flavor.
2. **Preserve creative tension.** Steel uses both an experimental art-critic perspective and a mainstream hustler/hitmaker perspective, then synthesizes them instead of allowing either to erase the other.
3. **Use evidence-based memory.** Approved artifacts, decisions, evaluations, and outcomes become memory. Raw conversation does not automatically become canon.
4. **Keep humans in control.** Persona canon, legal posture, public releases, external posts, spending, destructive actions, and production deployment require human approval.
5. **Use GitHub as canonical truth.** Buzz, Claude Projects, model chats, laptops, and research tools are working surfaces—not final authority.
6. **Verify every claim of completion.** Completion requires inspected diffs, passing tests or evals, artifact review, cost review, and a recorded outcome.

## First Product Slice: Steel Creative Session

1. Receive a song, hook, or verse objective.
2. Retrieve current Steel canon and relevant creative memory.
3. Generate candidate lyrics or concepts.
4. Evaluate candidates through the experimental critic and mainstream hitmaker perspectives.
5. Synthesize a ranked recommendation.
6. Obtain human approval.
7. Store the approved artifact, rejection reasons, scores, and provenance.

No autonomous social or release system is built before this vertical slice passes its acceptance tests.

## Authority Order

When instructions conflict, agents follow this order:

1. human-approved decisions recorded on `main`;
2. `PROJECT_CONTEXT.md` and `DECISIONS.md`;
3. current milestone and ownership in `EXECUTION_LEDGER.yaml`;
4. approved artist-bible and system documents;
5. source research and provenance records;
6. temporary branch notes and model conversation context.

## Current Boundary

The repository foundation and control plane are being established now. Production Steel behavior, persistent memory, Buzz integration, autonomous posting, and release automation are not yet approved or complete.
