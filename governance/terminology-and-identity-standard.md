# Steel Terminology and Identity Standard

**Status:** Approved operating standard  
**Effective date:** 2026-08-06  
**Owner:** Kirill Gabelev  
**Applies to:** public-facing identity, release titles, lyrics approved for release, production prompts, artwork text, social content, biographies, metadata, campaigns, and agent-generated outputs

## 1. Governing decision

Steel will not use **Gypsy**, **Gipsy**, **Gypsy King**, or confusingly similar language as a nickname, identity claim, public title, campaign concept, forward-looking lyric, or production instruction.

The music does not change. The terminology does.

## 2. Accurate project classification

Use:

> **AI-native virtual recording artist; human-directed, human-in-the-loop production.**

Do not describe Steel as autonomous, fully self-running, or independent of human creative control unless the underlying process materially changes and the legal/provenance framework is reviewed again.

## 3. Approved musical vocabulary

Preferred descriptive language includes:

- flamenco-inspired guitar
- Spanish nylon-string guitar
- percussive nylon-string guitar
- rasgueado patterns
- picado-style melodic runs
- Andalusian cadence
- minor-key Spanish harmony
- palmas-inspired rhythmic accents
- Golden Strings
- golden-string motif
- trap-flamenco
- Spanish guitar over modern trap drums

These terms describe musical characteristics without claiming an ethnic identity or imitating a named commercial artist.

## 4. Prohibited forward-use vocabulary

The following terms require removal or explicit governance review before public use:

- Gypsy
- Gipsy
- Gypsy King
- Gipsy Kings
- direct named-artist emulation instructions
- unsupported racial, ethnic, national, religious, or cultural identity claims
- autonomous AI artist
- fully self-running artist

## 5. Historical evidence exception

Historical lyrics and prompts must remain verbatim inside provenance and evidence records. They are facts about what occurred and must not be silently rewritten.

Every retained historical occurrence must be clearly labeled:

> **Historical evidence only — deprecated and not approved for future reuse or release without revision.**

A safe replacement must be stored separately from the original evidence.

## 6. Default replacement map

| Deprecated expression | Forward-use replacement |
|---|---|
| Gypsy strings | Golden Strings, flamenco-inspired strings, or Spanish nylon strings |
| Gypsy Strings With The Trap | Golden Strings With The Trap |
| Gypsy King | King of Strings or El Rey de las Cuerdas, subject to trademark clearance |
| Gipsy Kings-style | upbeat flamenco-pop guitar, handclap pulse, bright nylon-string ensemble |
| Spanish Romani-inspired guitar | flamenco-inspired Spanish nylon-string guitar |
| autonomous AI artist | AI-native virtual recording artist; human-directed, human-in-the-loop production |

## 7. Release gate

No release may pass clearance while a prohibited forward-use term remains in:

- the release title
- release lyrics
- public metadata
- cover artwork text
- public biography
- campaign copy
- final production prompt intended for reuse

Historical evidence may retain the original term only under the exception in Section 5.

## 8. Agent instructions

All agents working in Steelcore must:

1. Preserve raw historical evidence.
2. Generate safe normalized alternatives separately.
3. Flag prohibited forward-use terms.
4. Never convert an unknown into a guess.
5. Preserve evidence of Kirill's selection, direction, editing, rejection, and approval decisions.
