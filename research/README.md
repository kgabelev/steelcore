# Steelcore Research Topology

## Canonical records

- [Source register](source-register.csv) is the single repository-wide index for imported research evidence.
- [Open questions](open-questions.md) is the single repository-wide queue for primary-source verification, owner evidence, and counsel or specialist review.

Domain folders contain only their source-derived syntheses and supporting material. They do not maintain parallel source registers or open-question lists.

## Evidence rule

Every row in the source register is classified as evidence and marked `secondary_evidence_pending_primary_verification`. A source-derived synthesis may describe a report, risk, or hypothesis; it cannot establish approved legal, platform, distributor, product, or release policy.
