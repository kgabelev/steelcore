## What the uploaded report says

### Distributor differences

The report describes materially different positions among DistroKid, TuneCore, and CD Baby concerning fully AI-generated versus AI-assisted music. It emphasizes accurate AI-involvement disclosure, rights ownership, anti-impersonation rules, and anti-spam enforcement.

Steelcore must not encode these positions as permanent rules. The correct control is a dated release checklist that fetches or records the official policy in force at the time of submission.

### Platform enforcement

The report characterizes Spotify, Apple Music, and YouTube as generally using disclosure and enforcement mechanisms rather than blanket bans, with different approaches to labeling, manipulation detection, recommendation treatment, monetization, and penalties.

The durable design implication is that disclosure metadata, catalog quality, artist-profile substance, and stream integrity must be auditable.

### PRO registration

The report says partially AI-generated works may be treated differently from prompt-only works and stresses that human writers—not AI tools—must be identified as authors. This must be verified directly with the selected PRO before registration.

### Comparable AI-artist cases

The report uses three cases as strategic evidence:

- **Xania Monet:** commercial opportunity associated with human-written lyrics and public disclosure;
- **Breaking Rust:** commercial traction paired with uncertainty and risk around anonymous or undisclosed AI origin;
- **FN Meka:** severe cultural and reputational failure arising from persona design and insufficient equity review.

These cases support separate commercial, legal, cultural, and reputational review gates rather than one generic quality score.

## Steelcore design implications

1. Do not bulk-generate thin artist identities or duplicate catalogs.
2. Preserve a complete, consistent artist profile and provenance trail.
3. Require voice/likeness screening and cultural-grounding review.
4. Record AI involvement at the component level: lyrics, composition, vocals, instrumentals, artwork, and video.
5. Treat disclosure as an ongoing obligation that may be revisited after release.
6. Revalidate distributor, platform, and PRO rules immediately before action.
