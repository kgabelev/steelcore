# Steelcore Research Open Questions

## Primary-source verification required

1. What are the current official AI-content policies and upload fields for each candidate distributor?
2. What are the current official disclosure, labeling, recommendation, monetization, and enforcement rules for Spotify, Apple Music, and YouTube?
3. What are the current terms governing Suno outputs generated under each plan, and which terms applied on each historical generation date?
4. What are the current ASCAP and BMI registration instructions for works containing AI-generated material?
5. What exact US Copyright Office fields and evidence should be used for each Steelcore song's actual human contribution?
6. What EU AI Act transparency requirements apply to an independent US operator distributing synthetic audio into EU markets?

## Owner evidence required

1. Who wrote and edited each lyric version?
2. Which generation platform and subscription tier produced each audio or artwork artifact?
3. What generation dates, prompts, seeds, source audio, stems, and edits exist?
4. Which visuals or voices were intentionally modeled on references, and were any real people used?
5. Which songs, images, prompts, and personas are candidates versus rejected experiments?
6. What Suno links and audio variation IDs exist for Steel and Alma & Steel?

## Counsel or specialist review required before release

- copyright claim and limitation-of-claim strategy;
- voice and likeness screening;
- cultural-identity and reputational review;
- public AI disclosure statement;
- distributor and sync rights warranties;
- trademark clearance and filing strategy;
- entity choice and tax treatment.

## Research quality rule

A synthesized report may identify risks and hypotheses, but Steelcore does not promote a legal or platform rule into approved policy until the underlying current primary source is captured, dated, and reviewed.
