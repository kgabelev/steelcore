## What this pack is

A documented operating system for Steel / Steelz Music as a directed AI-assisted artist project: research, evaluation, multi-language experimentation, lyric authorship, style direction, model training/customization, and commercial release.

It separates four questions that are routinely collapsed:

| Question | Decision-maker | What "yes" requires |
|---|---|---|
| May this file be commercially exploited under the generator contract? | Suno Terms + plan + download record | Paid Pro/Premier at generation **and** a permitted download of that output |
| Is any part of the work copyrightable under U.S. law? | Copyright Act + USCO + courts | Human determination of sufficient expressive elements; prompts alone do not suffice |
| May a distributor deliver the recording? | Distributor + DSP policies | Disclosure, commercial-rights attestation, tool/dataset rules of that distributor |
| May mechanicals / publishing / PRO royalties be claimed? | MLC, PROs, publishers, USCO | A copyright owner of a human-authored musical work |

Contractual commercial rights and copyright are not the same thing. Suno states both facts: paid subscribers receive an assignment of Suno's rights in output, and Suno makes no warranty that copyright vests.

## Design rules

1. GitHub is canonical for text, SOPs, forms, and legal synthesis.
2. Drive holds binaries, screenshots, Suno license captures, RouteNote tickets, billing PDFs, and counsel packets.
3. Unknown facts stay marked `unknown` / `pending`. Do not backfill.
4. Canon classification is not release clearance.
5. Public posture: directed human authorship + disclosed AI assistance + open process documentation. No concealment of AI use.
