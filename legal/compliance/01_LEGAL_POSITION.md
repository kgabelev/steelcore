# Operating Legal Position — Directed AI Artist

**Version:** 0.1
**Date:** 2026-09-25
**Scope:** United States first. International rules differ and are not assumed.

This is an operating position for Steelcore documentation. It is not a legal opinion.

## 1. Project characterization

Steel / Steelz Music is a directed creative system. The human director supplies lyrics (full or partial), structure, style frameworks, evaluation criteria, remix selection, and catalog identity. Generative tools render audio and, in some cases, draft text. The project also runs formal evals (including multi-language tests such as Spanish bar-trading criteria and Russian-language song construction).

That characterization is evidence. It is not, by itself, a finding of copyright.

## 2. Controlling U.S. authorities (copyrightability)

| Source | Holding used here |
|---|---|
| U.S. Const. art. I, § 8, cl. 8; 17 U.S.C. § 102(a) | Copyright protects original works of **human** authorship |
| USCO policy statement, 88 Fed. Reg. 16,190 (Mar. 16, 2023) | Case-by-case; if a machine produces the traditional elements of authorship, those elements are not protected |
| USCO letter to The MLC (Apr. 20, 2023) | Works without sufficient human authorship have no copyright owner and are ineligible for § 115 blanket-license royalty distributions |
| USCO Copyright and Artificial Intelligence, Part 2 (Jan. 29, 2025) | Outputs protected only where a human determined sufficient expressive elements. Mere prompts are not sufficient. Use of AI as a tool does not bar copyright in the human-authored portions |
| Thaler v. Perlmutter, D.C. Cir. (Mar. 2025); Supreme Court declined review (Mar. 2026) | Human authorship is a statutory requirement |

Practical translation:

- A one-prompt, accept-as-is Suno file: no U.S. copyright in the generated composition or sound recording.
- Human-written lyrics that remain in the released work: candidate for copyright as a literary / musical-work contribution.
- Substantial human selection, arrangement, structural design, and modification of AI output: may support a claim in the human-authored aspects only.
- Detailed prompting, iteration, and curation, standing alone, are treated by USCO Part 2 as insufficient control over expressive elements.

## 3. Dual-layer rights model

Layer A — Generator contract — commercial exploitation permission
Layer B — Copyright statute — exclusive rights, registration, MLC/PRO
Layer C — Distributor / DSP — delivery, store eligibility, labeling
Layer D — Platform integrity — impersonation, fraud, volume, disclosure

A track may pass A and C and still fail B.

## 4. What this project will not claim without a completed dossier

- Copyright in raw AI audio, AI vocals, or AI-determined melody/harmony that the human did not author or substantially recast.
- Mechanical royalty ownership for wholly machine-authored musical works.
- That training a persona or writing a style prompt is itself copyright in the output.
- That open-sourcing SOPs creates a safe harbor.
