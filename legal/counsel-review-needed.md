# Counsel Review Needed

The items below remain unresolved until reviewed against current primary sources and the facts of the specific artifact or release.

## Before public artist launch

- trademark clearance and filing strategy for Steel, Alma & Steel, names, logos, and recurring source identifiers;
- legal review of the public AI disclosure statement;
- voice and likeness risk assessment for the approved voice and visual canon;
- cultural-identity and false-endorsement risk review;
- entity, contract, privacy, and community terms for any Buzz-facing public experience.

## Before first commercial release

- authorship and copyright claim strategy for the actual human and AI contributions;
- limitation-of-claim or disclaimer language, if applicable;
- confirmation of generation-platform rights and plan evidence for every material asset;
- distributor agreement, warranties, indemnities, and current AI-content policy;
- PRO registration treatment;
- sync-license warranty language;
- EU and other international disclosure obligations if distribution is global.

## Before using a real-person reference

- voice, likeness, publicity, privacy, and false-endorsement analysis;
- written consent or license scope;
- territory, duration, media, training, derivative-use, and revocation terms.

## Business and tax

- state-specific entity choice and maintenance requirements;
- classification of royalty, sync, merch, and service income;
- timing and economics of any S-corporation election;
- sales-tax, payroll, contractor, and international obligations.

## Evidence counsel should receive

- completed artifact provenance record;
- original and transformed files;
- human contribution evidence;
- AI session and platform-plan evidence;
- relevant terms snapshots;
- draft disclosures and metadata;
- intended territories, platforms, and contracts;
- unresolved items from `legal/risk-checklist.md`.

## Provenance fields counsel should not conflate

- `classification` records artifact state only: unresolved / evidence / experiment / candidate / canon.
- `approvals` records human decisions, including canon and release approvals.
- `release_clearance` records the separate clearance gate: cleared / pending / rejected / unresolved / unknown / not_applicable.
- `release` records actual distribution state: unreleased / scheduled / released / withdrawn / blocked.
